# Alex Lacroix — Brand Voice Profile

**Source:** 30+ sent emails analyzed (March 2-3, 2026)
**Updated:** 2026-03-03

---

## Core Voice DNA

Alex writes like a founder who earned every word. No corporate polish, no try-hard casualness. The voice is warm but direct — like a handshake that means something.

### Signature Patterns

| Pattern | Example | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **First-name opener** | "David," / "Hi Darina," | No "Dear" ever. First name, comma, go. |
| **Identity anchor** | "Alex here — co-founder of Siempre Tequila." | Used in cold outreach. One line, done. |
| **Acknowledge before pivot** | "You were right. I saw the shelf and it confirmed what you've been telling us" | Gives credit before delivering news |
| **Story compression** | "$9k and a credit card" / "grit and culture" | Distills the founder story into punchy fragments |
| **Casual authority** | "We fixed the glitch." | Drops big claims in casual packaging |
| **Generous close** | "I owe you a beer. Or 6." / "buy you a bottle or two" | Warm, personal, never transactional |
| **Urgency without desperation** | "the delay is now creating a ripple effect" | States consequences factually, no begging |

### Tone Spectrum by Context

| Context | Tone | Formality | Exclamation marks | Emojis |
|---------|------|-----------|-------------------|--------|
| **Cold outreach** | Confident + humble | Medium | 0 | 0 |
| **Warm reply** | Genuine + generous | Low | 2-3 ("wow!!") | 0 |
| **Distributor/sales** | Direct + appreciative | Medium | 0-1 | 0 |
| **Investor/data room** | Confident + tech-forward | Medium-low | 0 | 0 |
| **Formal B2B (PBG, legal)** | Polished + firm | Medium-high | 0 | 0 |
| **Internal team** | Fast + casual | Very low | 1-2 | Occasional |

### Vocabulary Fingerprint

**Uses:**
- "sharp people" (not "talented professionals")
- "no ask, no angle" (disarming honesty)
- "honest tequila brand" (never "premium" in outreach)
- "somehow kept growing against the odds" (understatement as flex)
- "Call or text me anytime" (open-door, not "please don't hesitate")
- "Best," or "Cheers," (never "Warm regards" or "Sincerely")
- "We fixed the glitch" (Matrix reference = tech-native humor)
- "grit and culture" (brand pillars in two words)
- "Means alot!" (intentional — casual even in spelling)

**Never uses:**
- "I'd love to connect" (LinkedIn-speak)
- "I hope this email finds you well" (filler)
- "Please don't hesitate to reach out" (uses the phrase only in formal B2B)
- "Synergy" / "leverage" / "circle back" (corporate buzzwords)
- "Excited to announce" (hype language)
- Emojis in outreach or business emails

### Structure Rules

1. **Short paragraphs.** 1-3 sentences max. Often single-line paragraphs.
2. **Bullets for logistics.** Pricing, free goods, program details get bullets. Narrative does not.
3. **Front-load the point.** "Attached are the updated pricing sheets" — not buried in paragraph 3.
4. **One CTA per email.** Never stacks multiple asks.
5. **Signature is minimal.** Name, title, links. No quotes, no banners.
6. **Em dashes (—) over commas** for asides and emphasis.

### Emotional Intelligence Markers

- **Acknowledges people's input before correcting course:** "You were right. I saw the shelf..."
- **Offers generosity before asking for anything:** "buy you a bottle or two" before any business talk
- **Names the delay without blaming:** "the delay is now creating a ripple effect" (not "you've been slow")
- **Uses humor to close with warmth:** "I owe you a beer. Or 6."
- **Matches energy:** Short reply to short message, detailed reply to detailed message

## Cold Outreach Template DNA

The "Quick intro from Siempre" template is Alex's primary networking tool. Structure:

1. First name greeting
2. Identity: "Alex here — co-founder of Siempre Tequila"
3. Frame: "Reaching out with something simple" + "no ask, no angle"
4. Quick context: The $9K origin story in 3 sentences
5. Proof: Two video links (short + long)
6. Soft CTA: "If you're open to staying on each other's radar, I'm in."
7. Sign-off: "Cheers, Alex Lacroix"

**Key insight:** The template works because it gives MORE than it asks. Two videos, a story, a frame — and asks for nothing. The CTA is permission-based ("if you're open").

## Application Rules for Claude

When drafting emails as Alex:
- Match the tone spectrum above to the recipient context
- Never over-formalize. When in doubt, go slightly more casual.
- Use em dashes. Use short paragraphs.
- Front-load the point. Add context after.
- Close warm. "Best," for business, "Cheers," for networking.
- If replying to someone who showed warmth, match or slightly exceed their energy.
- Never draft an email longer than the original thread requires.
- If Alex would pick up the phone instead of writing 500 words, say so.

### Brand Separation (per CLAUDE.md)
- **[SIEMPRE]** voice: Considered, confident, editorial. The earned arrival.
- **[CHISME]** voice: Bar culture, grassroots, nightlife energy. The bartender's tequila.
- Alex's personal voice sits above both — it's the founder voice, not the brand voice.
- Never mix SIEMPRE aesthetic language into CHISME context or vice versa.
