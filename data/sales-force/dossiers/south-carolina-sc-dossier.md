## 2. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

- South Carolina Department of Revenue — Alcohol Beverage Licensing Division
- Supplier registration required
- SC is unusual: mini-bottle law historically (1.7oz); now full bottle pour allowed (changed 2005)
- Brand/label approval required
- **No franchise law:** Distributor changes are legally uncomplicated
