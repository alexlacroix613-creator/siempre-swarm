## 2. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

**Province-Specific Compliance:**
- BC Liquor Distribution Branch (BCLDB) — wholesale control
- BC Liquor and Cannabis Regulation Branch (LCRB) — licensing
- Supplier must register as an importer/agent with BCLDB
- Product listing: Apply through BCLDB Connect Logistics or direct listing
- **CW Wines / Liquify** — historically used BC-specific distribution channels (verify current arrangement)

**Listing Types:**
- **General Listing:** Stocked in BC Liquor Stores provincewide
- **Private Liquor Store Listing:** Available to private stores, not necessarily BC Liquor Stores
- **Specialty Store Listing:** Specialty and VQA stores

**Promotional Rules:**
- More restrictive than Alberta
- In-store sampling at licensed retailers — permitted with approval
- Co-op advertising: BCLDB-regulated
- LRS (Liquor Retail Store) programs: private stores have more flexibility

**Escalation Triggers:**
- BCLDB listing lapse or SKU discontinuation
- Compliance issues with LCRB
- Supplier registration renewal
