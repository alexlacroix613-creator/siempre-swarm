## 2. Regulatory Framework

**License State Structure:**
Kansas operates as a license state under private distribution, regulated by the Kansas Alcoholic Beverage Control (ABC) board.

**Key Compliance Requirements:**
1. **Supplier Registration:** All suppliers must register with Kansas ABC before brand launch
2. **Label Approval:** All labels must be approved by state regulatory authority
3. **Sample & POS Rules:** Local requirements for samples and point-of-sale materials vary by jurisdiction
4. **Promotional Restrictions:** Local promotional rules are strict in some counties/cities

**Price & Posting Watchouts:**
- Must verify case FOB, discounts, and scans against distributor terms
- Bill-back arrangements require careful documentation against trade-practice rules
- No tied-house violations (federal FAA compliance mandatory)

**Local Market Nuances:**
- County and city retail rules can override state rules in some jurisdictions
- Chain headquarters coverage agreements can restrict store-by-store placement
- Must run formal Joint Business Plans (JBPs) with distributor partners
- Pricing must be policed actively to prevent grey market activity

**Escalation Triggers - Contact Counsel When:**
- Custom incentives or display pay-to-play arrangements proposed
- Retailer service offers that may constitute tied-house violations
- Distributor termination threats or conflicts emerge
- Exception terms requested outside standard contract framework

**Primary Regulatory References:**
- Kansas ABC Board regulations
- Federal Alcohol Administration Act (tied-house provisions)
- Internal: USA Management Sheet 2025, WIP 2025 Control State Pricing Calendar
