## 2. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

### AGLC (Alberta Gaming, Liquor and Cannabis)
- **Primary Regulator:** Provincial monopoly body controlling spirits distribution and licensing
- **Product Registration:** All SKUs must be registered with AGLC
- **Wholesale Access:** AGLC Gateway/Portal system manages orders and pricing updates
- **Pricing Authority:** AGLC sets mandatory wholesale pricing structures (different for standard vs. small producers)

### Licensing & Approval Workflow
1. Product SKU registration with AGLC (UPC/SCC code validation required)
2. Wholesaler/distributor appointment approval
3. Pricing submission and approval through AGLC Gateway
4. Compliance reporting on depletion/sales through AGLC systems

### Small Producer Status
- **Eligibility:** Reduced markup structure due to smaller production volume
- **Benefit:** Lower wholesale pricing improves retail competitiveness
- **Margin Profile:** Currently manageable (24% on Plata, pending optimization)

### Key Contact Points
- AGLC Portal: Gateway system for order placement and pricing updates
- AGLC Pricing: Mandatory pricing submission process for any wholesale price changes
