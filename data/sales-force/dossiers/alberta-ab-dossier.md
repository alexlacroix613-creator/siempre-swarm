## 2. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

**Province-Specific Compliance:**
- Alberta Gaming, Liquor and Cannabis (AGLC) governs
- Supplier must hold AGLC Import Agent or Manufacturer license
- Product registration via AGLC Connect (online portal)
- Brand/label approval required — SKU registration in AGLC system
- Alberta Import Agent (AIA) model: supplier works through an AIA (import agent) who holds the AGLC license

**Pricing:**
- Alberta is price-competitive — retailers set their own prices (unlike control provinces)
- AGLC sets a "cost" (laid-in cost) from which retailers markup
- More flexibility on promotions vs. Ontario/BC

**Promotional Rules:**
- Volume discounts: Permitted at distributor/retailer level
- In-store promotions: Allowed
- Tastings: Permitted with AGLC approval
- Co-op advertising: Permitted
- More promotional freedom than most Canadian provinces

**Escalation Triggers:**
- AGLC product registration lapse
- Import agent relationship issues
- AR balance with AGLC or distributor
