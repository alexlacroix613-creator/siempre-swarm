## 2. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

**State-Specific Compliance:**
- Utah Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control (DABC) governs
- All spirits retail through DABC state stores (off-premise) — no private retail
- On-premise: Licensed restaurants/clubs — tiered license types (dining club, bar establishment, etc.)
- **COLA requirement:** Utah requires separate DABC product approval. Federal COLA alone is NOT sufficient.
- High-proof licensing requirements

**DABC Listing Types:**
- **Listed Item:** Regular DABC listing — stocked in state stores
- **Special Order:** Customer or retailer requests specific items — not shelf stock
- **Club Item:** Available to licensed clubs only

**Promotional Rules:**
- Very limited. DABC tightly controls promotional activity.
- Tastings: Extremely restricted. Must comply with DABC tasting permit rules.
- No standard three-tier promotional mechanics (DAs, billbacks) — control state
- On-premise brand building is the primary marketing avenue

**Escalation Triggers:**
- ⚠️ **COLA expiration** — triggers immediate delisting from DABC
- Any compliance event with DABC
- License type questions from on-premise accounts
