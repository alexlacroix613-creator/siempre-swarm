## 1. MARKET OVERVIEW

**Province**: Manitoba (MB)
**Regulatory Body**: MLLC (Manitoba Liquor and Lotteries Corporation)
**Population**: ~1.4M | Winnipeg metro ~850K
**Market Type**: Government monopoly distribution through MLLC warehouse
**Distribution Model**: Central purchasing body → Government retail stores + Private licensees
**Siempre Status**: Plata & Reposado listed | **Anejo NOT listed** (priority gap)

### Key Context
Manitoba operates one of Canada's most restrictive control-state models. MLLC manages all purchasing decisions, pricing, and wholesale distribution through a centralized warehouse. Direct applications are limited; tenders are the primary pathway (quarterly cycles). The market is smaller than BC or Ontario but strategically important as a home market.
