## 2. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

**State-Specific Compliance:**
- Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission (OLCC) — full control
- All spirits sold at OLCC-licensed stores (state stores + contract stores)
- Supplier must hold OLCC Importer/Brewery/Winery/Distillery license
- Product listing approval required — OLCC Product Registration
- Pricing: Set by OLCC markup formula. Supplier submits FOB, OLCC applies markup.
- **COLA requirement:** Oregon requires label approval via OLCC before listing

**Promotional Rules:**
- On-premise: Supplier reps can do staff trainings, tastings at licensed venues (with permits)
- Off-premise: Limited promotional activity (OLCC controls retail)
- No performance DAs, billbacks, or distributor-style programs (no private distributor)
- Supplier-funded promotions must be OLCC-approved

**Escalation Triggers:**
- OLCC listing expiration or delisting
- Label change requiring re-approval
- On-premise compliance (tasting permit issues)
