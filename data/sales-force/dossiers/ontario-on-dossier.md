## 2. Regulatory Framework

**AGCO (Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario):**
- Oversees all alcohol licensing and product registration in Ontario
- Spirits must be registered with AGCO before any sales
- License requirements for producers/importers
- Product approval required for all SKUs

**LCBO (Liquor Control Board of Ontario):**
- Crown agency - Ontario's government-run spirits retailer
- All spirits retail sales must flow through LCBO for off-premise consumption
- Direct supplier program for qualifying producers/importers
- Product must be listed on LCBO inventory to generate sales
- Order-to-delivery cycle managed through LCBO Gateway portal
- Depletion data available via LCBO public inventory (limited) + Analyticsmart (internal)

**Listing Process (LCBO):**
1. Product registration with AGCO
2. Product submission to LCBO for consideration
3. LCBO product committee review
4. Listing approval (if successful)
5. Direct PO issuance via LCBO Gateway
6. Fulfillment and delivery to LCBO warehouses
7. Placement to retail stores based on demand

**On-Premise Channel (AGCO-Licensed Bars/Restaurants):**
- Private distributors can supply bars, restaurants, hotels
- Dandurand is Siempre's primary on-premise distributor
- AGCO licenses the establishments; distributors manage supply

**Provincial Rules & Escalation Triggers:**
- All marketing/promotional material requires AGCO pre-approval
- No direct sales to consumers (Crown monopoly for off-premise)
- Tied-house rules apply (no exclusive dealing arrangements)
- Pricing must be registered with LCBO (no dynamic pricing)
- Distributor agreements must comply with AGCO guidelines
- Any distributor changes require notification to AGCO

**Key Regulatory Reference:**
- AGCO Main: https://www.agco.on.ca/
- LCBO Supplier Portal: https://lcbo-vendor-portal.launchpad.cfapps.us10.hana.ondemand.com
