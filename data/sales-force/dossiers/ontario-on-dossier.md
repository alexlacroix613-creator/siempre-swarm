## 6. PRICING ARCHITECTURE

**LCBO Pricing Model (Control Province):**
- Supplier negotiates LCBO cost price → LCBO applies fixed markup → sets consumer shelf price
- **LCBO markup:** ~58% on spirits (LCBO takes ~36.7% of shelf price back to province)
- Typical calculation: Consumer price = LCBO cost ÷ (1 - LCBO markup %)
- Siempre current LCBO cost prices: **VERIFY with Trajectory/Rick** (prior Noble Estates agreement may need renegotiation)
