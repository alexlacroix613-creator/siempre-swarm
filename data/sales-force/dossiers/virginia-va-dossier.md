## 2. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

### CRITICAL CONTROL STATE STRUCTURE

Virginia ABC is a state board that controls all spirits wholesale and retail commerce. This creates materially different operational requirements vs. open states.

**Key Regulatory Elements:**
- **Listing Cycles:** Fixed submission windows (September 2026 listed cycle confirmed in email chain dated 3/23-3/27/26)
- **Board Submissions:** All new items, price changes, and delists require formal applications
- **State Warehouse Rules:** All spirits must pass through Virginia ABC warehouse before retail placement
- **Bailment/Special Order Status:** Non-listed items available only through special order with margin restrictions
- **Price Filing Requirements:** Price changes must be filed in advance; different rules apply for listed vs. special-order items
- **Registration Timing:** Registration deadlines are non-negotiable and drive quarterly/annual planning

### Compliance Calendar (2026)
- **September 2026 Listing Window:** CRITICAL DEADLINE - Retail listing submission deadline was 3/27/26 (confirmed in email from Brian Guilbeau)
- **Pricing Alignment:** Price support/promotion strategies must be synchronized with ABC calendar
- **Board Communication:** State Manager or broker submits all compliance documents through formal channels

### Price & Posting Watchouts
- Price changes often require advance notice (e.g., 30-60 days)
- Listed items allow direct pricing; special-order items have built-in markup restrictions
- Retail price setting is state-controlled (unlike open states)
- Price elasticity in control states is constrained by state margin rules

**Escalation Points for Compliance/Counsel:**
- Any listing issue or delist risk
- Non-standard sample or POS material request from retailer
- Change to price-support structure or promotional strategy
- Broker/distributor alignment drift on deadlines or submissions

### Pricing Calculator Framework
Virginia ABC uses a state-mandated pricing formula that calculates retail shelf price based on:
- Case cost (FOB)
- Bottles per case
- Spirit proof
- Container size markup
- ABC markup percentage (dependent on category: distilled spirits, wine, vermouth, mixers)
- Special order premium (if not regularly listed)

**Formula Application:**
1. Enter FOB cost + case configuration
2. System applies proof/size multiplier
3. ABC markup % applied
4. Special order surcharge added (if applicable)
5. Result rounded up to nearest .99

### Price Change Management
- **Lead Time:** 30-60 days advance notice required for ABC filing
- **Approval Cycle:** ABC board review + distributor confirmation
- **Effective Date:** Synchronized with ABC calendar (not immediate)
- **Lock-in Period:** Typically 3-6 months minimum (control state standard)
