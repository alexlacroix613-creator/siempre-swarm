## 2. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

**State-Specific Compliance:**
- California Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control (ABC) governs
- No franchise law complications for standard license states
- Supplier registration: ABC License Type 14 (importer), Type 17 (beer/wine/spirits importer), or agent arrangement through Winebow
- Brand/label approval: TTB federal + CA state label registration
- DAs: Permitted — no blanket restrictions on distributor allowances in CA standard markets
- Volume discounts: Permitted in standard structure

**Promotional Rules:**
- Post-off programs: Permitted
- DA/OPA (Off-Premise Allowance): Permitted
- In-store tastings: Permitted at licensed retailers with proper permits
- Tied-house: Strict CA tied-house laws — cannot fund or pay retailer fixtures without exception permits. Consult before any retailer co-op.

**Escalation Triggers:**
- Any tied-house adjacent arrangements (retailer exclusives, co-op payments)
- ABC compliance events
- Label or compliance issues
