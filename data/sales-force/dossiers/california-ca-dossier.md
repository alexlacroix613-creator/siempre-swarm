## 2. Regulatory Framework

**Key Compliance Watchouts (from 50-State Matrix):**
- **Supplier Registration:** Must confirm supplier registration with state before launch/activation
- **Label & Brand Approvals:** Brand label applications required pre-activation
- **County/City Rules:** Significant variance by county and city; retail rules differ locally
- **Chain Headquarters Rule:** Chain HQ location (many in CA) influences statewide purchasing and pricing approvals

**Price/Posting Watchouts:**
- Verify case FOB pricing for accuracy and compliance
- Track discounts applied to distributor; audit for consistency
- Confirm scans and bill-back arrangements match agreed pricing
- Monitor custom incentives and display pay programs for regulatory alignment

**Local Market Nuances:**
- County and city retail rules create complexity; cannot assume statewide uniformity
- Chain headquarters in CA (Costco HQ in Sunnyvale, Albertsons operations) have outsized influence
- On-premise vs off-premise split varies significantly by territory
- Regional wine/spirits culture in different CA regions requires territory-specific strategy

**Escalation Triggers:**
- Any custom incentive program not pre-vetted by compliance
- Display pay or retail setup fund discussions
- Retailer setup/slotting fee proposals
- Distributor margin disputes or unilateral discount claims
- County-level regulatory questions (route to legal)

## 6. Historical Performance

**6-Year Depletion Data (Cases) - 2019 through 3/25/2024:**

| SKU | Total Cases (6yr) | # Purchases |
|-----|-------------------|-------------|
| **CA Total** | **6,288** | **2,142** |
| Siempre Anejo | 2,507 | 994 |
| Siempre Plata | 2,305 | 1,374 |
| Siempre Reposado | 1,188 | 931 |
| Siempre Rebel Cask | 145 | 70 |
| Siempre Exclusivo Vivo | 104 | 94 |
| Siempre Supremo | 39 | 61 |

**6-Year Did Buys Data (Store Placements) - 2019 through 3/25/2024:**

| SKU | Total Did Buys | # Placements |
|-----|----------------|-------------|
| **CA Total** | **642** | **2,142** |
| Siempre Plata | 508 | 1,374 |
| Siempre Anejo | 433 | 994 |
| Siempre Reposado | 443 | 931 |
| Siempre Exclusivo Vivo | 54 | 94 |
| Siempre Rebel Cask | 33 | 70 |
| Siempre Supremo | 34 | 61 |

**Performance Trend Analysis:**

1. **Volume Trajectory:**
   - CA cases distributed across 6 years; no data shows 2019-early 2024 baseline
   - 2,142 purchase events over 6 years = ~357 per year on average
   - Strong retail placement velocity (642 store did-buys for 2,142 purchases = high repeat velocity)

2. **Growth Pattern:**
   - Plata is highest volume SKU (2,305 cases; 1,374 placements)
   - Anejo strong performer (2,507 cases; 994 placements)
   - Reposado consistent (1,188 cases; 931 placements)
   - Newer SKUs (Rebel Cask, Exclusivo Vivo, Supremo) ramping but lower base volume

3. **Market Maturity Indicators:**
   - High did-buy count vs. cases suggests repeat retail placement and reorders
   - Plata conversion ratio: 2,305 cases / 1,374 placements = 1.68 cases per placement (good repeat)
   - Anejo: 2,507 / 994 = 2.52 cases per placement (strong!)
   - Indicates mature, sticky shelf presence in CA

4. **Growth Rate Estimate:**
   - Year-over-year breakdown not provided in 6-year data; requires access to annual breakdown files
   - Suggest review of annual files (2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 YTD) for precise CAGR
