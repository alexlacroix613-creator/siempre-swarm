## 2. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

**State-Specific Compliance:**
- Washington State Liquor and Cannabis Board (WSLCB) governs
- No franchise law complications for standard license states
- Brand registration: TTB federal + WSLCB state registration
- **Important note:** WA does NOT have a state-controlled wholesale system (unlike ID, MT). It is a true private three-tier state, but with historically unusual retail rules (privatized in 2012 after long state control history).
- DAs: Permitted
- Volume discounts: Permitted

**Promotional Rules:**
- Post-offs: Permitted
- DAs: Permitted — verify with distributor
- Costco: WA is unique — Costco is a licensed spirits retailer in WA (due to 2012 privatization). Costco WA is a major spirits channel. Distributor relationship with Costco buyer matters.

**Escalation Triggers:**
- WSLCB compliance events
- Costco WA program approvals (significant sales potential)
