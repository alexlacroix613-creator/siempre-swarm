## 6. Historical Performance

**6-Year Depletion Data (Cases) - 2019 through 3/25/2024:**

| SKU | Total Cases (6yr) | # Purchases |
|-----|-------------------|-------------|
| **WA Total** | **1,041** | **98** |
| Siempre Reposado | 669 | 36 |
| Siempre Plata | 349 | 49 |
| Siempre Anejo | 24 | 51 |

**6-Year Did Buys Data (Store Placements) - 2019 through 3/25/2024:**

| SKU | Total Did Buys | # Placements |
|-----|----------------|-------------|
| **WA Total** | **29** | **98** |
| Siempre Anejo | 22 | 51 |
| Siempre Plata | 25 | 49 |
| Siempre Reposado | 21 | 36 |

**Performance Trend Analysis:**

1. **Volume Profile:**
   - WA total cases: 1,041 over 6 years = ~173 cases/year average
   - Much lower absolute volume than CA (CA: 6,288 cases vs. WA: 1,041)
   - Lower number of purchase events (WA: 98 purchases vs. CA: 2,142)
   - Indicates smaller market or early-stage penetration

2. **Skew Analysis - KEY INSIGHT:**
   - **Reposado dominates:** 669 cases (64% of total WA volume)
   - Plata secondary: 349 cases (34%)
   - Anejo minimal: 24 cases (2%)
   - **vs. CA ratio:** CA has more balanced Anejo/Plata/Reposado mix
   - **Interpretation:** WA market favors Reposado; premiumization opportunity with Anejo

3. **Retail Placement Velocity:**
   - WA did-buys very low: 29 total placements vs. 98 purchase events
   - This is opposite of CA (which showed high did-buys relative to purchases)
   - **Interpretation:** WA retailers are NOT repeating orders; one-time trials
   - **Risk:** Low stickiness suggests retail shelf placement is volatile/experimental
   - **Opportunity:** Repeats are rare; margin/sell-through improvement could drive growth

4. **Purchase Event Concentration:**
   - 98 purchase events over 6 years = ~16 per year
   - Much lower frequency than CA's ~357 per year
   - Suggests smaller territory, fewer active accounts, or lower sales velocity

5. **Growth Rate Estimate:**
   - 6-year aggregate data doesn't show year-by-year breakdown
   - Estimated volume per year: 173 cases = modest baseline
   - Recommend pulling annual breakdowns (2019-2024 YTD files) to assess CAGR
   - If trending up, market may be in growth phase; if flat, indicates saturation

**Comparison to CA:**
- CA: 6,288 cases vs. WA: 1,041 = CA is 6x larger
- CA: 2,142 purchases vs. WA: 98 = CA has 22x more purchase events
- CA repeat ratio strong; WA repeat ratio weak
- **Strategic Implication:** WA is underpenetrated relative to market size; major growth opportunity if distributor/retail execution improves
