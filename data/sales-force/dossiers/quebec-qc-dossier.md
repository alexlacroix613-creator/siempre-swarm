## 2. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

**Province-Specific Compliance:**
- Société des alcools du Québec (SAQ) governs all spirits
- Supplier must register as Supplier with SAQ (Régie des alcools, des courses et des jeux — RACJ for licensing)
- French labeling: **MANDATORY** — all products must have French-language label compliance (Bill 101 / Loi sur la langue française). Labels must be in French or bilingual.
- eLLIS: Quebec uses the **eLLIS tender system** for listing applications (shared with other Canadian provinces)
- Product listing through SAQ appel d'offres (tender) process

**SAQ Listing Types:**
- **Régulier (Regular):** Permanent listing in SAQ stores province-wide
- **Spécialité (Specialty):** Available in select SAQ Sélection and Signature stores
- **Importation privée (Private Import):** Special order for restaurants

**Promotional Rules:**
- Very restrictive — SAQ controls pricing and promotion
- SAQ-managed promotions: Featured items, in-store displays
- On-premise: Supplier can do staff trainings, BYOW (Bring Your Own Wine) restaurant relationship building
- No standard three-tier promotional mechanics

**Escalation Triggers:**
- SAQ delisting risk (critically low inventory → may trigger SKU review)
- French labeling compliance failure
- eLLIS tender window deadlines
