## 2. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

**State-Specific Compliance:**
- Louisiana ATC (Alcohol and Tobacco Control) governs
- La. R.S. Title 26 — foundational liquor statute
- La. R.S. 26:80 — trade practice restrictions (high alcoholic content)
- Supplier registration required
- Brand/label approval required
- No mandatory price filing/posting

**Promotional Rules:**
- Volume discounts: Allowed
- DAs/Post-offs: Allowed (standard practice, though Siempre chose not to use them)
- Free goods: Restricted ("something for nothing" prohibited, but buy-X-get-Y structured as volume discounts OK)
- Tastings/samplings: Widely permitted with permits
- Consignment sales: Prohibited
- Credit terms: Regulated collection timeframes

**Escalation Triggers:**
- Any tied-house questions
- Free goods structuring
- Reyes transition compliance — ensure all agreements transfer cleanly
