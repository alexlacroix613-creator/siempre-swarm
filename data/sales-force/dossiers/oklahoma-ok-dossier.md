## 2. Regulatory Framework

**License State Structure:**
Oklahoma operates as a license state under private distribution, regulated by the Oklahoma Alcoholic Beverage and Tobacco Board (OABTB).

**Key Compliance Requirements:**
1. **Supplier Registration:** All suppliers must register with OABTB before brand appointment
2. **Label Approval:** All labels must meet Oklahoma-specific requirements
3. **Sample & POS Rules:** Strict local restrictions on promotional materials and samples
4. **Promotional Restrictions:** County-level variations in promotional allowances

**Price & Posting Watchouts:**
- Case FOB, discounts, and scans must be verified against distributor terms and state trade-practice rules
- Bill-back documentation critical for compliance
- Federal tied-house regulations strictly enforced
- No territorial price discrimination allowed under state law

**Local Market Nuances:**
- County and city retail rules override state in some cases
- Chain headquarters sometimes restrict location-by-location authority
- Indian Nation territories have separate regulatory authority in some cases
- Must maintain active JBP with distributor to enforce pricing and execution

**Escalation Triggers - Contact Counsel When:**
- Custom incentive arrangements proposed
- Display pay-to-play agreements discussed
- Retailer service arrangements that could constitute tied-house violations
- Distributor termination or relationship conflicts emerge
- Unusual margin or pricing exception requests
