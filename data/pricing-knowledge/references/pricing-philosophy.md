# Pricing Philosophy: Siempre Spirits

## "Margin = Oxygen"

At Siempre Spirits, margin is the lifeblood of the business. We do not compromise on our margin floor (30%) because it provides the "oxygen" needed for growth, marketing, and operations.

## Core Principles

1. **No Desperation Discounting**: We do not discount to hit short-term volume targets if it compromises our long-term brand value or margin floor.
2. **Avoid Discount Quicksand**: Once you start discounting, it's hard to stop. We establish and defend price floors from day one.
3. **90-Day Payback Mindset**: Every investment in pricing or promotion must have a clear, measurable path to payback within 90 days.
4. **Casamigos Gap Opportunity**: We position our core line (Plata, Repo, Añejo) to compete effectively in the premium space, leveraging gaps left by competitors like Casamigos.

## Strategic Positioning

- **Premium Quality, Premium Price**: Our production methods (NOM 1414, 1438, 1137) justify our premium SRP targets.
- **Floor Pricing**: We don't just set a target; we set a floor and defend it.
